# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

ChurchCore contributors, maintainers and community members are expected to communicate with integrity, professionalism and mutual respect. ChurchCore serves churches and the people in their care — including children, donors and people receiving pastoral care — and the way we work together should reflect that trust.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and church and theological traditions.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, including review findings.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the wider community and the churches served by ChurchCore.
- Handling church, member, child and donor data with care, and never sharing it outside its purpose.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
- Posting real personal data (members, children, donors, care records) in issues, pull requests, logs or screenshots.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions — and when an individual is officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers privately (the repository owner listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS)). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.
